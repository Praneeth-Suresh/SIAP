Safety Constraints and Guardrails

Safety is not a postscript to capability; it is constrained optimization from first principles.

Constrained control formalization

Model policy selection as a constrained MDP: [ \max_{\pi}\ J_R(\pi)=\mathbb{E}\pi!\left[\sum_t\gamma^t r_t\right] \quad\text{s.t.}\quad J}(\pi)=\mathbb{E\pi!\left[\sum_t\gamma^t c\right]\le d_j. ] The Lagrangian form [ \mathcal{L}(\pi,\lambda)=J_R(\pi)-\sum_j\lambda_j\big(J_{C_j}(\pi)-d_j\big) ] supports dual optimization and transparent trade-off tuning.

Three-theory braid

  1. Constrained MDP theory. Utility-safety trade-offs become explicit and optimizable [51].
  2. Control barrier functions (CBFs). Safety can be imposed as forward-invariance constraints (h(x)\ge0) at runtime [52].
  3. Normative governance frameworks. Constitutional and risk-management frameworks encode institution-level constraints beyond model internals [29] [53].

How to study this formally

Report Pareto fronts of task return versus violation probability, plus time-to-escalation under unsafe trajectories. Layered guardrails should be tested for correlated failure modes, not in isolation. Constraint-aware autonomy is not anti-innovation; it is innovation that survives contact with reality.
