Alternative rewrite

Embodiment is where grand theories meet stubborn reality. A plan that ignores sensor noise, action latency, and delayed rewards is not bold; it is nonphysical.

Pass 1 - Migrated ideas from current main topic

The current topic models embodiment with state dynamics (x_{t+1}=f(x_t,u_t)+w_t) and observations (o_t=g(x_t)+v_t), then treats control as long-horizon utility optimization under actuator/safety bounds. Its braid combines Kalman-style estimation, RL decision dynamics in MDP/POMDP form, and practical infrastructure coupling through latency and non-stationarity [36] [3] [4] [2].

Pass 1 - Storyline outline (paragraph-by-paragraph)

Paragraph 1 opens with a physicality thesis: every agent is embodied because every action touches a delayed, lossy, capacity-limited environment.

Paragraph 2 introduces noisy sensing and delayed actuation as geometric distortions of the control loop.

Paragraph 3 develops state estimation and feedback control as the mechanism for converting raw observations into stable action [36] [41].

Paragraph 4 links immediate control choices to long-horizon regret through Bellman recursion and policy optimization [5] [3].

Paragraph 5 resolves with resilience: saturation, permission boundaries, and lag-aware replanning as first-class design constraints rather than implementation afterthoughts.

Pass 2 - Research synthesis: equations, theories, and connective tissue

Equation set: delayed dynamics (x_{t+1}=f(x_t,u_{t-d_u})+w_t), delayed observation (o_t=g(x_{t-d_o})+v_t); Kalman predict-update (\hat x_{t|t-1}=A\hat x_{t-1|t-1}+Bu_{t-1}), (K_t=P_{t|t-1}H^\top(HP_{t|t-1}H^\top+R)^{-1}), (\hat x_{t|t}=\hat x_{t|t-1}+K_t(o_t-H\hat x_{t|t-1})) [36]; LQR feedback (u_t=-K\hat x_t) from Riccati structure [41]; RL objective (J(\pi)=\mathbb{E}\pi[\sum_t\gamma^t r_t]) and Bellman optimality (V^\star(s)=\max_a{R(s,a)+\gamma\sumP(s'|s,a)V^\star(s')}) [3] [4] [5]; saturation constraint (|u_t|\infty\le u). External equation check in pass 2 used underactuated control notes for dynamic programming and LQR (DP, LQR). Connection narrative: estimation provides situational awareness, control provides immediate correction, and Bellman planning provides long-run coherence.
