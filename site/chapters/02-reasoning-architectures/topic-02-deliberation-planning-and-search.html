Deliberation, Planning, and Search

Deliberation is the art of spending computation now to avoid regret later.

Formal planning frame

Let (G=(V,E)) be a search graph over decision states. A plan is a path (p=(v_0,\dots,v_T)) minimizing [ J(p)=\sum_{t=0}^{T-1} c(v_t,v_{t+1}) + \Phi(v_T), ] with admissible heuristic (h(v)\le h^{}(v)) for A optimality.

In stochastic domains, planning becomes belief-space optimization or Monte Carlo tree search over sampled rollouts.

Three-theory braid

  1. Heuristic graph search. A* gives optimality guarantees under admissibility/consistency assumptions [8].
  2. Symbolic planning. STRIPS-style operators and planning-domain models encode action preconditions/effects for compositional plan construction [9] [10].
  3. Anytime and tree-search control. UCT/MCTS and anytime planning trade compute for solution quality under real-time budgets [44] [45].

How to study this formally

Benchmark plan quality against compute budget, replan frequency, and irreversible-error rate. Analyze sensitivity to heuristic misspecification and environment volatility. The best planner is not the one with the fanciest tree; it is the one whose assumptions fail gracefully.
